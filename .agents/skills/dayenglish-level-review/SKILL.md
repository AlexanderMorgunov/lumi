# Review language and puzzle meaning

Adapted from OxfordEnglish exercise-logic-review. Read [GDD](../../../docs/GDD.md) and [chapter 1](../../../docs/chapter-01.md). Review actual maps and conditions; the chapter outline is not a solvable map.

Solve the level first without relying on its answer key. Identify what each character knows, which objects are uniquely identifiable, what the task requests and which constructions are natural. Compare all natural alternatives with accepted data; either accept them or make the scene unambiguous. First mention can use the; repeated mention alone does not force it.

Check a/an by the spoken onset, not spelling; keep chapter 1 within explained count nouns: singular a/an/the and the authored apples/eggs plural cases with no article or the. Reject a/an with plural nouns; do not generalize bare plurals to every plural context. Optional article slots must be explicitly authored; singular lessons still require their article. Word meaning must be introduced before assessment. Do not call phrases sentences or encode articles as universal world commands.

Check form, context and goal separately, including feedback for a valid phrase that does not solve the room. Confirm Undo restores selected/known objects while attempt and revealed-hint history persists. Confirm hints, translation, silence or subscription do not alter the accepted answer.

When rewriting lesson text, use [dayenglish-learning-copy](../dayenglish-learning-copy/SKILL.md) for natural character speech and clear instructions. Preserve terms, examples and rule boundaries; simpler wording must not change the set of valid answers or disclose the solution in an independent task.

Report concrete findings as level ID + field/state + severity + observed issue + valid alternative or fix. Do not claim audio was checked without listening, maps solved without execution/solver evidence, or learning validated without learners. Local deterministic tests cover specified cases; a methodological review covers natural interpretations.
